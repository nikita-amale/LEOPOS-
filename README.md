# LeoPOS – Point of Sale & Product Management System

LeoPOS is a Point of Sale (POS) and product management system designed to support business operations such as product management, purchases, sales, quotations, invoices, payments, returns, customers, and suppliers.

I contributed to the development and maintenance of the system, mainly working on **Product Purchase** and **Invoice Generation** functionality, along with backend debugging and bug fixing.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

LeoPOS provides functionality for managing day-to-day Point of Sale operations, including:

- Product Management
- Product Purchases
- Sales Management
- Invoice Generation
- Quotations
- Payments
- Returns
- Customer Management
- Supplier Management
- Database Operations

My contribution focused primarily on **Product Purchase and Invoice Generation**, along with identifying and resolving backend and functional issues.
